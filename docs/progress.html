Retromod progress

Recent releases moved beyond simple redirects into mixin handler repair, Forge and NeoForge bridges, world-generation fixes, and broader Minecraft 26.2 support.

Working today

  • Fabric, NeoForge, and Forge builds through Minecraft 26.2
  • Fabric intermediary and Forge SRG remapping
  • Selected mixin signature repairs and removed-API bridges
  • Forge 26.2 event-bus compatibility
  • Offline transform, AOT cache, verification, Probe, and community reports

Still limited

  • Custom renderers and coremods such as Create, Flywheel, Veil, and OptiFine
  • Large Forge-to-NeoForge migrations outside the common registration path
  • Many pre-1.13 mods after Minecraft's flattening
  • Mixins whose target logic or local-variable layout was redesigned
  • Automatic OpenGL-to-Vulkan translation
